About the City

       

          Zakho is a beautiful and ancient city in the Duhok Governorate of the Kurdistan Region of Iraq[cite: 3].            Built along the banks of the Little Khabur River, it has been a vibrant center of trade, culture, and hospitality for thousands of years.        

       

          The city covers an urban area of about 70 km2, with warm summers reaching 40°C and green, pleasant springs.

Cultural Terms

     
       
Pira Delal
       
A historic stone bridge built with massive carved rocks without modern cement.
       
Khabur River
       
The famous river that flows through the heart of Zakho.
